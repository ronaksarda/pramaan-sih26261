network

Colleges that share faculty, photos, files or land

One application cannot show these. Pramaan joins every college's evidence, so a teacher on three payrolls, one fire NOC used twice, or one plot of land shown by two colleges becomes visible. Click a college to open its case.

inspection plan

Which colleges should an expert team visit first?

Self-disclosure approvals are checked only after approval (APH 2.5(c)), and inspection teams are few. Pramaan ranks colleges by how little of their claim is proven, and puts colleges that share faculty, photos, files or land into the same visit.

analyse a document

Check any certificate on its own

Drop in a PDF or a photo of a document. Pramaan checks its signature, every saved version, values hidden under boxes, edited areas on scans, what the issuing office says, and whether any college already submitted the same file.

issuer simulator · state fire department

What happens if a certificate is revoked after approval?

This page stands in for the fire department's own system. Revoke a NOC and Pramaan re-checks every approved college with its issuers, alerts the approver, and marks the public approval record as under review.
